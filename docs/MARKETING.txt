BlueFairy Forms — Marketing Content
=====================================


3-SENTENCE SUMMARY
------------------

BlueFairy Forms is a zero-asset-bloat WordPress forms plugin that gives developers a Gutenberg block and shortcode for contact forms with no jQuery, no third-party scripts, and no bloat. Every submission is stored in a privacy-safe database with HMAC-hashed IPs, consent snapshots, a full audit log, and rate limiting — and the plugin integrates automatically with BlueFairy Anti-Spam for invisible honeypot and time-trap protection. Open source, GPL-2.0-or-later, requires PHP 8.1+ and WordPress 6.4+.
